Model fixes: I found that a few FBX files had export problems, and they're now fixed.

- Oil Barrel: the FBX was empty, with no model inside it.
- Airplane, Hammer Claw, and Trash Can: the FBX files weren't linked to their textures, so they came into engines like Godot as plain white. They now link to their textures correctly.
- Medkit: some faces on the front of the cross were facing the wrong way, so the cross disappeared in engines like Godot. It now shows correctly.

That was my export mistake. If you downloaded any of these before today, please grab them again. Sorry about that.
