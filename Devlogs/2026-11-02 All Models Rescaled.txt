Every CC0Tree model now uses real-world scale. I found that the sizes were inconsistent (a pen was 20 units long while a medkit was 3), so objects looked wrong side by side in a game engine. All 28 models are now fixed.

- 1 unit = 1 meter. A pen is 15 cm, a crate 60 cm, a katana 1 m, a pine tree 6 m.
- The origin is at the center of the base and every model sits on the ground, so it drops into a scene without any adjusting.
- Object scale is 1, with no hidden scaling.

Your imports will look different from before: most models are smaller and a few are larger. If you downloaded any models earlier, please re-download them.
